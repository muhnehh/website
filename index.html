Muhammed Nehan

Ajman University, UAE

Hey there! I’m Muhammed Nehan. I’m currently a junior undergraduate student at Ajman University in the UAE studying Artificial Intelligence. I’m a 6x hackathon winner.

I mainly work on on-device ML inference and retrieval-augmented generation - building systems that run fast without cloud dependencies. I have worked on various projects in these areas including my paper on DustAdapt, edge rerankers, and RAG evaluation frameworks. You can find more about my work here.

I have also worked on interesting technical projects across agentic pipelines, routing optimization, and developer tooling - most recently LoomMCP, a context compiler for AI coding agents built at the Claude Code Hackathon. Feel free to reach out via email if you find my work interesting!
