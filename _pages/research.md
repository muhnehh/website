### Research Interests

- **On-device inference** - ONNX quantization, model compression, latency-aware design
- **Distribution shift & uncertainty** - test-time adaptation, conformal prediction, drift detection
- **Retrieval-augmented generation** - evaluation frameworks, reranker design, agentic retrieval
- **Edge-cloud hybrid systems** - selective offloading, confidence-gated computation

I work independently as an undergraduate researcher without lab affiliation or external funding.
